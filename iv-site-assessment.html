Peripheral IV Site Assessment

Learn how to assess a peripheral IV site for common complications.

IntermediateStep-by-step guideEducational resource
Educational notice: This page contains educational content only. Always follow your institution's current policies, professional standards and applicable clinical guidance.

Quick overview

Peripheral IV site assessment checks the insertion site and surrounding tissue for complications.

Why is it important?

Early recognition of infiltration, phlebitis or other complications supports patient safety.

Equipment

🧤
Gloves
🩹
Dressing supplies when required
💉
IV equipment as applicable

Step-by-step guide

1

Inspect

Inspect the IV insertion site.

Look for changes such as redness, swelling, leakage or other abnormal findings.

2

Assess

Assess the patient and site.

Ask about pain or discomfort and assess the site according to local policy.

3

Check

Check the IV system as required.

Review patency and the prescribed infusion without unnecessary manipulation.

4

Act

Report or manage concerns appropriately.

Follow local escalation and device-management guidance when abnormalities are identified.

Safety and precautions

✓ Follow current institutional guidance.
✓ Confirm patient-specific requirements.
✓ Use appropriate infection-prevention measures.
✓ Escalate unexpected or concerning findings through the appropriate clinical pathway.

Documentation

Document relevant findings or actions according to the applicable clinical documentation requirements.
