About LearningNursing.com

Simple, visual and practical nursing education for students.

Our goal

LearningNursing.com was created to make nursing education easier to understand, more practical and accessible to students. Each procedure is broken into clear steps, with a short explanation for each one.

The creator

LearningNursing was created by K. Jashikesh, a nursing student who wanted study material that is clear, organised and easy to revise from.

What you will find

  • Step-by-step procedure guides with equipment lists, safety points and documentation reminders
  • Study notes across nursing fundamentals, pathology, pharmacology, medical-surgical nursing, anatomy, care plans, research and NCLEX preparation
  • A nursing calculator for dose, IV rate, drop rate, BMI, MAP and unit conversions

How to use this site

LearningNursing.com is an educational resource. It does not replace your course material, your institution's policies, or the guidance of your clinical supervisors. Where a guide cites WHO or CDC sources, the links are listed at the bottom of the page.
