Intramuscular Injection

Learn the principles behind intramuscular medication administration.

IntermediateStep-by-step guideEducational resource
Educational notice: This page contains educational content only. Always follow your institution's current policies, professional standards and applicable clinical guidance.

Quick overview

An intramuscular injection delivers medication into muscle tissue.

Why is it important?

Intramuscular administration is one of several medication routes used in clinical practice.

Verify
