Temperature Measurement

Learn the basic principles of measuring body temperature.

BeginnerStep-by-step guideEducational resource
Educational notice: This page contains educational content only. Always follow your institution's current policies, professional standards and applicable clinical guidance.

Quick overview

Temperature measurement provides an assessment of the body's temperature.

Why is it important?

Temperature can contribute important information to a patient's overall assessment.

Equipment

🌡️
Appropriate thermometer
🧤
Personal protective equipment when indicated

Step-by-step guide

1

Prepare

Prepare the equipment and patient.

Select an appropriate measurement method and equipment according to current guidance.

2

Position

Position the thermometer correctly.

Use the technique appropriate to the device and measurement site.

3

Measure

Obtain the temperature.

Follow the manufacturer's instructions and local clinical procedure.

4

Record

Record the result.

Document the result and measurement method accurately.

Safety and precautions

✓ Follow current institutional guidance.
✓ Confirm patient-specific requirements.
✓ Use appropriate infection-prevention measures.
✓ Escalate unexpected or concerning findings through the appropriate clinical pathway.

Documentation
