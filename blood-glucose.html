Capillary Blood Glucose

Learn the basic workflow for capillary blood glucose monitoring.

IntermediateStep-by-step guideEducational resource
Educational notice: This page contains educational content only. Always follow your institution's current policies, professional standards and applicable clinical guidance.

Quick overview

Capillary blood glucose testing provides a point-of-care measurement of blood glucose.

Why is it important?

Glucose monitoring can support assessment and management of patients with altered glucose levels.

Step-by-step guide

1

Prepare

Prepare the meter and supplies.

Check the device, test strip and required infection-control supplies.

2

Prepare Site

Prepare the selected finger or approved site.

Use the approved site-preparation technique for the device and local policy.

3

Test

Obtain and apply the blood sample.

Use the manufacturer's instructions and current clinical procedure.

4

Document

Record and respond to the result.

Document the reading and follow the relevant escalation or treatment pathway when indicated.

Safety and precautions

✓ Follow current institutional guidance.
✓ Confirm patient-specific requirements.
✓ Use appropriate infection-prevention measures.
✓ Escalate unexpected or concerning findings through the appropriate clinical pathway.
