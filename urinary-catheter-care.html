Urinary Catheter Care

Learn essential principles of caring for a patient with an indwelling urinary catheter.

IntermediateStep-by-step guideEducational resource
Educational notice: This page contains educational content only. Always follow your institution's current policies, professional standards and applicable clinical guidance.

Quick overview

Urinary catheter care aims to maintain a closed system and reduce preventable complications.

Why is it important?

Appropriate catheter care supports patient comfort and infection prevention.

Equipment

🧤
Gloves
🧼
Approved cleansing supplies
🗑️
Waste container

Step-by-step guide

1

Assess

Assess the catheter system and patient.

Check comfort, drainage and the integrity of the catheter system.

2

Maintain

Maintain the drainage system appropriately.

Keep the system secure and follow current infection-prevention guidance.

3

Provide Care

Provide routine catheter hygiene.

Use the approved technique and avoid unnecessary manipulation of the system.

4

Document

Document care and observations.

Record relevant findings, urine output when required and any concerns.

Safety and precautions

✓ Follow current institutional guidance.
✓ Confirm patient-specific requirements.
✓ Use appropriate infection-prevention measures.
✓ Escalate unexpected or concerning findings through the appropriate clinical pathway.

Documentation

Document relevant findings or actions according to the applicable clinical documentation requirements.
