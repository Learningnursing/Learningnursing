Oral Care

Learn a structured approach to oral hygiene for dependent patients.

BeginnerStep-by-step guideEducational resource
Educational notice: This page contains educational content only. Always follow your institution's current policies, professional standards and applicable clinical guidance.

Quick overview

Oral care maintains oral cleanliness, comfort and tissue health.

Why is it important?

Regular oral care supports comfort and can reduce avoidable oral complications.

Equipment

🪥
Toothbrush or oral-care equipment
💧
Water or approved solution
🧤
Gloves
🗑️
Waste container

Step-by-step guide

1

Assess

Assess the patient's oral condition.

Observe the mouth, lips, teeth, tongue and relevant symptoms before care.

2

Prepare

Prepare the patient and equipment.

Position the patient safely and explain the procedure.

3

Clean

Provide oral hygiene using the appropriate technique.

Clean the mouth gently according to the patient's needs and current clinical guidance.

4

Finish

Reassess comfort and document as required.

Ensure the patient is comfortable and record relevant findings or care provided.

Safety and precautions

✓ Follow current institutional guidance.
✓ Confirm patient-specific requirements.
✓ Use appropriate infection-prevention measures.
✓ Escalate unexpected or concerning findings through the appropriate clinical pathway.

Documentation

Document relevant findings or actions according to the applicable clinical documentation requirements.
