Nasogastric Tube Feeding

Review the nursing principles involved in enteral feeding through an NG tube.

IntermediateStep-by-step guideEducational resource
Educational notice: This page contains educational content only. Always follow your institution's current policies, professional standards and applicable clinical guidance.

Quick overview

Nasogastric feeding delivers prescribed nutrition or fluids through a feeding tube.

Why is it important?

Safe enteral feeding requires appropriate patient assessment, tube verification and monitoring.

Equipment

🧪
Prescribed feed
🧴
Enteral feeding equipment
🧤
Gloves
💧
Flush solution as prescribed

Step-by-step guide

1

Assess

Assess the patient before feeding.

Check the patient's condition and follow the current enteral-feeding protocol.

2

Verify

Verify tube placement using the approved method.

Do not proceed until tube placement has been confirmed according to current clinical guidance.

3

Feed

Administer the prescribed feed safely.

Use the prescribed method, rate and precautions for the patient.

4

Monitor

Monitor tolerance and document care.

Observe for complications and record the feed, flushes and patient response as required.

Safety and precautions

✓ Follow current institutional guidance.
✓ Confirm patient-specific requirements.
✓ Use appropriate infection-prevention measures.
✓ Escalate unexpected or concerning findings through the appropriate clinical pathway.

Documentation

Document relevant findings or actions according to the applicable clinical documentation requirements.
