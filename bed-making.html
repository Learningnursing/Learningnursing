Bed Making

Learn the basic principles of preparing a clean patient bed.

BeginnerStep-by-step guideEducational resource
Educational notice: This page contains educational content only. Always follow your institution's current policies, professional standards and applicable clinical guidance.

Quick overview

Bed making involves preparing a clean, safe and comfortable patient bed.

Why is it important?

A properly prepared bed supports comfort, hygiene and patient safety.

Equipment

🛏️
Appropriate bed
🧺
Clean linen
🧤
PPE when indicated

Step-by-step guide

1

Prepare

Prepare the environment and equipment.

Gather the required linen and follow local infection prevention procedures.

2

Remove

Remove used linen appropriately.

Handle used linen according to institutional infection-control procedures.

3

Make

Apply clean linen correctly.

Follow your nursing school's approved bed-making technique.

4

Check

Check the finished bed.

Ensure the bed is clean, safe and appropriate for the patient's needs.

Safety and precautions

✓ Follow current institutional guidance.
✓ Confirm patient-specific requirements.
✓ Use appropriate infection-prevention measures.
✓ Escalate unexpected or concerning findings through the appropriate clinical pathway.

Documentation

Document relevant findings or actions according to the applicable clinical documentation requirements.
