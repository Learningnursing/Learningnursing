Safe Patient Transfer

Learn the safety principles for transferring a patient between surfaces.

IntermediateStep-by-step guideEducational resource
Educational notice: This page contains educational content only. Always follow your institution's current policies, professional standards and applicable clinical guidance.

Quick overview

Safe patient transfer uses assessment, appropriate equipment and coordinated movement.

Why is it important?

Good transfer technique reduces avoidable injury risk for patients and staff.

Equipment

🛏️
Bed or transfer surface
♿
Wheelchair when required
🦯
Mobility aid when indicated
🧤
PPE as required

Step-by-step guide

1

Assess

Assess mobility and transfer needs.

Consider the patient's condition, strength, cognition and required assistance.

2

Prepare

Prepare the environment and equipment.

Position equipment safely and remove avoidable environmental hazards.

3

Transfer

Perform the transfer with appropriate assistance.

Use the approved technique and equipment for the patient's assessed needs.

4

Reassess

Reassess the patient after transfer.

Ensure the patient is safe, comfortable and positioned appropriately.

Safety and precautions

✓ Follow current institutional guidance.
✓ Confirm patient-specific requirements.
✓ Use appropriate infection-prevention measures.
✓ Escalate unexpected or concerning findings through the appropriate clinical pathway.

Documentation

Document relevant findings or actions according to the applicable clinical documentation requirements.
