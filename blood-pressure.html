Blood Pressure Measurement

Understand the basic process of measuring blood pressure.

BeginnerStep-by-step guideEducational resource
Educational notice: This page contains educational content only. Always follow your institution's current policies, professional standards and applicable clinical guidance.

Quick overview

Blood pressure measurement assesses the pressure of circulating blood against arterial walls.

Why is it important?

Blood pressure is an important component of routine patient assessment.
