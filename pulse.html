Pulse Assessment

Learn the basic process of assessing a peripheral pulse.

BeginnerStep-by-step guideEducational resource
Educational notice: This page contains educational content only. Always follow your institution's current policies, professional standards and applicable clinical guidance.

Quick overview

Pulse assessment involves evaluating the arterial pulse at an appropriate site.

Why is it important?

Pulse assessment can provide useful information about cardiovascular status.

Equipment

⏱️
Watch or timer
📝
Documentation system or chart

Step-by-step guide

1

Prepare

Prepare the patient and explain the assessment.

Use an appropriate pulse site and follow your institution's assessment procedure.

2

Locate

Locate the pulse.

Use the appropriate fingers and anatomical location according to clinical teaching.

3

Assess

Assess the pulse.

Assess the characteristics required by your clinical procedure.

4

Record

Document the findings.

Record the relevant assessment findings accurately.
