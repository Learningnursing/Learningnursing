Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Overview

LearningNursing.com is an educational website. It does not have user accounts, does not sell anything and does not show advertising. This page explains what information is and is not collected when you use it.

Information you give us

The site has no sign-up or comment forms. If you email us at jasi.bala2003@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use these only to reply to you and to improve the site, and we do not share them with third parties.

The nursing calculator

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The values you enter are not sent to us or stored by the site.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may collect technical information such as IP addresses and browser details when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Search engines

We use Google Search Console to see how the site appears in Google Search. This gives us aggregated search information, not information about individual visitors.

Cookies and analytics

The site does not set its own tracking cookies. If we add analytics or advertising in future, this page will be updated first, and a cookie notice will be added where required.

External links

Some pages link to external websites such as WHO and CDC. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites.

Children

The site is intended for nursing students and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: jasi.bala2003@gmail.com.
